Strategic outcomes
The developments that defined Months 4 & 5
  • Visibility & content performance. 4,662 impressions and 177 likes across Posts 13–20, holding a 3.95% combined engagement rate. Post 14 delivered the widest reach of the cycle (1,299 impressions, 43 likes, 4 comments), pulling in around 40 named engagers, while Post 20 led on engagement rate (5.75%).
  • Network penetration, sharply up. Of the 549 senior professionals targeted across 22 named automotive/GCC companies, 216 are now confirmed as accepted connections — a 39.3% match rate, the highest conversion recorded for this campaign to date, out of 222 total accepted connections captured in the network export.
  • Target-list precision. 216 of the 222 accepted connections (97.3%) trace directly back to the structured target list, showing the outreach is landing almost entirely within the intended automotive-engineering and GCC leadership audience rather than drifting into unrelated contacts.
  • Seniority quality (sample view). Among a 71-person representative sample of the 216 matches (with full role, company and profile detail), 48 (67.6%) sit at Director-level or above — including 14 C-Suite/CXOs such as COOs, CHROs and an MD/CEO, and senior VPs across ABB, Wipro and MBRDI.
  • Market mapping. Strongest conversion in the sample was at Wipro and Accenture (9 confirmed each), with deep penetration also at Capgemini, ABB, MBRDI and HCLTech — the core of Vikas's target universe across Tier-1 automotive suppliers and Global Capability Centres.
  • Inbound opportunities. Direct inbound outreach surfaced a Director-level opportunity at EPAM Systems and a discussion on multiple senior-level openings — including a Senior Program Manager role — at KPIT, both arriving without outbound sales motion.
